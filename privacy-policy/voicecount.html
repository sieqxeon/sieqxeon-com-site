App Information, Privacy Policy & Support

App Information

Voice Word Count (VoiceCount)

Voice Word Count is a simple application that counts spoken words in real time using voice recognition. It can be used for speech practice, presentations, language learning, or hands-free counting.

  • Features
    • Real-time voice recognition
    • Counts spoken words automatically
    • Hands-free operation
    • Simple and lightweight interface

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Voice Word Count (also known as VoiceCount, "the app") is provided by sieqxeon ("we", "the developer"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Microphone and audio

The app uses the microphone only to recognize the words you speak while you are using the app (when you tap Start, or when you register a word by voice). Audio is processed on your device by an on-device speech recognition engine (Vosk). Audio is not recorded, stored, or sent to our servers or to any third party.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Data stored on your device

The words you register, their counts, and your settings (language, sound, theme, font size, and purchase status) are stored only on your device. We cannot access them. This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage.

Advertising

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as the advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), and device and app diagnostics in order to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. The app requests non-personalized ads. For details, see Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card or account details; we only receive confirmation that the purchase was made.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data, and we do not store user data on our servers. The only third-party data collection is by the advertising service described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
