Voice Word Count

Released

iOS / Android

Voice Word Count is a simple app that counts how many times you say a word you've registered. Just start speaking and the app will automatically count each time you say that registered word, using speech recognition. Because it works hands-free, you can use it while working, walking, or doing other tasks.

Android: Released iOS: Coming soon

Kehai is a quiet way to check in on a family member living alone, or someone in your care. It reports whether their phone showed any sign of use during the day — screen taps, walking — with no location tracking and no camera monitoring.
